Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Capital Bump is a personal application operated by Mitchell Ballinger for private use. This policy describes how Gmail access is used when you authorize the app.

What we access

With your consent, Capital Bump requests the gmail.readonly scope. That allows the app to list and read email messages. It cannot send, delete, or modify mail.

How that data is used

  • Bank alert emails (for example MSUFCU eAlerts) are parsed into transactions and balances.
  • Results are stored in databases on machines I control (a home server spool and a desktop app database).
  • Gmail content is not sold, advertised against, or shared with third parties.

Where credentials live

OAuth tokens are stored as a credentials file on the home server, with restricted file permissions. They are not embedded in the public source repository.

Retention

Parsed financial records stay in the application databases until I delete them. You can revoke Capital Bump’s Gmail access at any time at Google Account permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the email address associated with the Google Cloud project / OAuth consent screen for Capital Bump.
